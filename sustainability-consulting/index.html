What sustainability consultants do

  • Measure your carbon footprint across scopes 1, 2 and 3.
  • Set targets and a practical reduction plan.
  • Answer customer and tender sustainability questionnaires.
  • Prepare sustainability or ESG reports.
  • Support certifications such as ISO 14001 or B Corp.

Frameworks you’ll hear about

  • GHG Protocol: the most widely used standard for measuring greenhouse gas emissions.
  • Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi): validates emissions-reduction targets.
  • ISO 14001: the international standard for environmental management systems.
  • B Corp: a certification for social and environmental performance, run by B Lab.
  • Reporting rules: the EU’s Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD), whose scope and timing have been revised, and the UK’s Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR) for larger companies.

Avoid greenwashing

Environmental claims must be accurate and backed by evidence. The UK CMA’s Green Claims Code, the US FTC Green Guides and EU consumer law all target misleading green claims. A good consultant will help you say only what you can prove.

What affects the cost

Business size, the number of sites and suppliers, data availability, the framework or certification, and whether you need software. Footprint projects are often fixed fees; ongoing support is usually a retainer.

Frequently asked questions

What are scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions?
Scope 1 is direct emissions from what you own, scope 2 is from purchased energy, and scope 3 covers your wider value chain, such as suppliers and customers.
Do small businesses need to report on sustainability?
Mandatory rules mostly target larger companies, but small suppliers are often asked for data by larger customers.
Is carbon offsetting enough?
Most frameworks put reducing emissions first. Offsets, if used, should be high quality and described accurately.
