Fixed fee, hourly or retainer? How professional services are priced

Two quotes for the same job can look nothing alike. Understanding pricing models helps you compare fairly, whether you’re buying or selling.

By the ProSourced editorial team · 5 September 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Jakub Żerdzicki on Unsplash

  2. How to compare quotes on different models

The main models

ModelHow it worksGood whenWatch out for
Fixed feeOne price for a defined scopeThe scope is clearExtra charges for changes
Hourly or day ratePay for time spentScope is uncertainCosts that run on
RetainerMonthly fee for ongoing work or accessRegular, repeat needsUnused hours
Value or success-basedFee linked to an outcomeOutcomes are measurableDisputes over what counts

How to compare quotes on different models

  1. Use the same brief for every provider.
  2. Ask hourly quoters for an estimate of total hours and a cap.
  3. Ask fixed-fee quoters exactly what’s included, and the rate for extras.
  4. For retainers, check what happens to unused hours and the notice period.
  5. Add expenses, taxes (such as VAT or sales tax) and software costs to every quote.

For professionals: choosing your model

Fixed fees reward efficiency and are easier for clients to approve. Hourly billing protects you when the scope is unclear. Retainers smooth out your income. Work out your floor with the hourly and day rate calculator before you quote.
