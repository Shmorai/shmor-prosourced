Who we’re looking for

  • Accounting, bookkeeping and payroll firms.
  • HR consultants, recruiters and employer-of-record providers.
  • Management consultants and fractional executives.
  • Web designers, developers, branding studios and marketing agencies.
  • Business lawyers, IT providers, translators and virtual assistant agencies.

Listings and partnerships are coming soon. To register your interest or discuss advertising, contact the Shmor.ai partnerships team.

Win more work from every lead

  • Reply quickly and answer the brief point by point.
  • Show credentials, registrations and insurance up front.
  • Give a clear price and say exactly what’s included.
  • Share two or three case studies from similar clients.
