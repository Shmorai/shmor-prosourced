Why billable hours matter most

Selling, admin, learning and invoicing all take time you can’t bill. If you plan on billing every working hour, your rate will be too low. Be realistic about how many hours a week clients will actually pay for.

Frequently asked questions

Which tax rate should I use?
Use an estimate of the overall tax and social contributions on your profit where you live. An accountant can give you a more accurate figure.
Should I quote hourly or a day rate?
Hourly suits small or unpredictable tasks. Day rates suit consulting and project work. Fixed fees reward efficiency, if the scope is clear.
