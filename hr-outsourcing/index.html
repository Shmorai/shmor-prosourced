What outsourced HR covers

  • Employment contracts, offer letters and staff handbooks.
  • Policies for leave, remote work, conduct, data protection and more.
  • Employee relations: grievances, disciplinaries, performance and absence.
  • Hiring processes, onboarding and right-to-work checks.
  • Pay structures, appraisals and training plans.
  • Choosing and setting up HR software.

Employment rules change at every border

  • United States: mostly at-will employment, with federal laws plus state and city rules on things like paid leave and pay transparency.
  • United Kingdom: written statements of employment particulars from day one, statutory leave and pay, and the ACAS Code of Practice for disciplinary and grievance procedures.
  • European Union: EU directives set minimums (for example on working time), and each country adds its own rules. Works councils are common in some countries.

When a dispute may turn into a claim, bring in an employment lawyer. See legal services.

Ways to buy HR help

  • Ad hoc: pay for a project such as a handbook, or for advice on one issue.
  • Retainer: a monthly fee for advice and documents on demand.
  • Fractional HR lead: a senior HR professional for a set number of days a month.
  • PEO (US) or employer of record (abroad): HR bundled with payroll and benefits.

Credentials and questions to ask

Look for professional membership such as CIPD (UK and Ireland), or SHRM-CP/SHRM-SCP and HRCI certifications (US). Then ask:

  • Which countries’ employment rules do you advise on?
  • Do you carry professional indemnity insurance?
  • What’s included in a retainer, and how quickly do you respond?
  • Will you work with our lawyer if a matter escalates?

Frequently asked questions

When should a small business outsource HR?
Common triggers are hiring your first employees, growing past the point where the founder can handle people issues, hiring in a new country, or facing a difficult employee situation.
Can an HR consultant represent us at an employment tribunal?
Rules on representation vary by country. For claims and tribunals, most businesses use an employment lawyer, often alongside their HR consultant.
Is outsourced HR cheaper than an in-house HR manager?
For small teams it often is, because you only pay for the time you use. Try our free in-house vs outsourcing calculator with your own figures.
