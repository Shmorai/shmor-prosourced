Business services in the United Kingdom

What to know before you hire accountants, payroll, HR, lawyers and other business professionals in the United Kingdom.

Photo: Henrique Ferreira on Unsplash

Key rules and credentials in United Kingdom

  • Accountants: chartered accountants (ICAEW, ICAS), ACCA and CIMA members. Check the body’s register.
  • Payroll: PAYE with Real Time Information reporting to HMRC, plus workplace pension auto-enrolment.
  • HR: CIPD membership is the main professional standard. ACAS publishes free codes and guidance.
  • Solicitors: regulated by the SRA in England and Wales. Scotland and Northern Ireland have their own law societies.
  • Company formation: register with Companies House.
  • Contractors: the off-payroll working rules (IR35) can apply.

  • Software development: Web and mobile apps, integrations, custom systems and dedicated dev teams.
