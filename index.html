Frequently asked questions

Is ProSourced free for businesses?
Yes. Browsing, comparing and using our free tools costs nothing, and there’s no obligation to hire.
Which countries does ProSourced cover?
Our guides are written for businesses in the United States, United Kingdom and European Union, with notes for Canada, Australia and other markets. Many services, such as design, marketing and virtual assistants, can be delivered remotely from anywhere.
Does ProSourced vet the professionals?
No. We’re an introduction and comparison service. Each guide explains which credentials to check and where to verify them, and you agree terms directly with the professional you choose.
How does ProSourced make money?
Some links are referral links, so partner platforms may pay us a fee when we introduce you, at no extra cost to you.
